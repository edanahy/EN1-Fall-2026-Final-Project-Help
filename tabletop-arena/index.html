1. Connect to your SPIKE Prime

  1. Download hub_stream.py to a program slot on the hub (with the SPIKE App), then close the SPIKE App.
  2. Turn the hub on, then click Connect and pick your hub (Chrome / Edge only).
  3. Click Start program (or press the hub's button) and watch the numbers move on the Calibrate screen.

Keyboard mode (when no hub is connected)

← → move   ↑ ↓ aim turret   Space fire. These fake the two motors and the force sensor, so calibration works too.

Full screen

Click Full screen (top right) to hide the browser's tabs and address bar; Esc leaves it. On the Play screen the menu bar fades out when the mouse is still; move the mouse to bring it back. Use Rotate screen if the monitor is upside down or you are viewing from another side.

2. Calibrate

Move the robot / turret to the position described, then click the button next to it. The game then maps everything between those two positions onto the screen.
