Two SPIKE hubs, one web page

Use Chrome or Edge. Close the SPIKE App first. Click Connect in each panel and pick a different hub each time (tip: give your hubs different names first so you can tell them apart in the picker). Each panel is its own SpikeBLE.SpikeHub object, so the two hubs never share data. The "msgs/s" number in each panel shows how many Bluetooth messages per second that hub is delivering (handy for spotting lag). Tested with two real hubs from file://; more than two has not been tried.
